5 Dashboard Areas

Operational Performance In One Dashboard

Focused dashboard areas provide a consolidated operational view without duplicating the functionality of Odoo's underlying business applications.

📊

Executive Summary

Management Overview
  • Headline Metrics: Revenue, Net Profit, Cash, Overdue Invoices, and Sales Orders.
  • Working Capital: Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable, and DSO.
  • Operational Pulse: Inventory alerts, pending deliveries, receipts, and project activity.
  • Management Navigation: Direct access to relevant Odoo records.
💰

Finance & Accounting

Financial Visibility
  • Revenue & Expenses: Financial performance for the selected period.
  • Profitability: Gross and net profitability metrics.
  • Cash & Banking: Available bank and cash balances.
  • Receivables & Payables: AR, AP, overdue invoices, and DSO.
📈

Sales & CRM

Sales Activity & Pipeline
  • Sales Orders: Confirmed sales orders across selected periods.
  • Pipeline: Open opportunity value and sales activity.
  • Win Rate: Opportunity conversion information from CRM.
  • Customer Performance: Top customers by confirmed sales revenue.
📦

Inventory & Operations

Stock & Fulfillment
  • Inventory Valuation: Current inventory value from Odoo stock data.
  • Low Stock: Products below configured minimum quantities.
  • Operations: Pending incoming receipts and outgoing deliveries.
  • Purchasing: Overdue purchase orders and reorder information.
📁

Project & Operations

Tasks & Execution
  • Overdue Tasks: Open project tasks that have passed their deadline.
  • Tasks Due Today: Current project deliverables requiring attention.
  • Project Monitoring: Active projects and operational task information.
  • Odoo Drilldown: Open the underlying projects for further review.
